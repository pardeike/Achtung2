Achtung! gives you tighter control over colonists when a normal right-click order is not enough.

It is made for the busy moments: raids, fires, wounded pawns, half-built defenses, and work that must happen now instead of after a snack break.

What it adds:

- force work so pawns stay on important jobs
- order several colonists at once
- move drafted colonists in lines and formations
- reposition groups without rebuilding the whole shape
- command work outside normal allowed areas when you really need it

It is a practical control mod, not a new storyteller or content pack. You still play RimWorld, you just get sharper tools for telling your colony what to do.
